Pre-scoring clarification, 2026-10-28

Known detector abstention (e.g. insufficient grades) keeps the frozen -infinity
score. A process failure is different from a known abstention. A missing required
negative score contributes +infinity to calibration, so a failed computation
cannot make a threshold more permissive. Report such calibration counts.
For future negative outcomes, report observed alarms and both the number of
incomplete pairs and an upper bound treating unresolved nonalarms as alarms.
For positives, missing embedding/replay/detection cannot count as a successful
usable detection; retain it in the planned-attempt denominator.
Paired intervals use source-key averaged outcomes conditional on fitted
thresholds, with 5000 filename-cluster draws and 5000 circular chronological
moving-block draws of length ceil(sqrt(number of selected test transitions)).
They are descriptive sensitivity intervals, not calibrated confidence claims
under arbitrary temporal dependence. These rules precede new scoring outcomes.
