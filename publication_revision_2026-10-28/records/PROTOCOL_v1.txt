FSE continuation: maintenance evidence and detector recovery
Date: 2026-10-28. Status: active research, not submission-ready.

Preserve paper/, major_revision_2026-10-27/, and all historical outputs.
Run new empirical computations on inner. Public benchmark sources only.
First correct SEW verdicts, including MIN_EVIDENCE, from frozen row-level data.
Do not count this correction as a scientific contribution.

Research question: how much maintenance-induced detection loss is attributable
to changed addressing, and how much can a detector recover without the original
code, edit log, or changes to the deployed embedder?

Development cohort: the existing Python SEW outputs. They have been inspected
previously and cannot serve as an untouched confirmation set. All results below
are explicitly exploratory. Subsequent languages/benchmarks require a separate
frozen protocol before their outcomes are examined.

Detector recovery candidates: replace the observed global context tuple by
nonnegative integer tuples within L1 distance 1 or 2. These sets depend only on
the suspect code, never on the original context or on detector outcomes.
Compare official detection, unadjusted minimum p (diagnostic), multiplicity-
adjusted minimum p, and negative-only calibrated scan. All retain >=5 grades.
Oracle original context is a diagnostic ceiling, never a deployable comparator.
Keep original, formatter, comment stripping and dead-return conditions; the
last is a synthetic mechanism intervention, not sampled developer maintenance.

Report full-denominator detection, functional-and-detected rate, negative alarm
rates, grade coverage, paired task-cluster intervals and runtime. Calibrate on
negative programs only, splitting by semantic task across keys and edits.
Compare original-only frozen calibration with edit-matched calibration; the
latter assumes knowledge of the edit distribution and is a diagnostic reference.
No claim of fixed-key distribution-free validity from a random-key argument.

Near-neighbor boundary: synchronization is already studied by SynMark, robust
text watermarks and earlier code obfuscation work. SEW already groups repeated
grades and calibrates style frequencies. CREST already tests maintenance attack
chains. Novelty must lie in verified recovery capability and actionable measured
limits beyond these methods, not in naming synchronization or adding languages.

Deliverable target: revised English paper + supplement + runnable source and
traceable tables. Prefer JSS/EMSE subject to verified scope and no mandatory APC.
Do not call a working draft a high-quality submission until its claims, baselines,
functional checks, statistical comparisons and full PDF have been reviewed.
